Combined campaign + maze

This is a feedback preview integrating the existing opening and outer world with Marc's maze. It is not the public release and has not completed the final end-to-end polish audit.

Start here

  • Normal game entry: review the opening, recovery, optional training and existing world. Complete the shallow Bucky/grapple/three-plate puzzle, then swim through its opened doorway marked MAZE ENTRANCE. The laboratory is a different destination leading to Tethys; its victory is not required to enter the maze.
  • Focused maze entrance: skips the opening for faster geometry, map and puzzle review. This starts a diagnostic party, not your earned campaign party. It is not proof of normal progression or boss readiness.

WASD swims; Space/Shift change depth; Tab switches diver. In the maze, L opens/closes the map. Left/Right selects hallways and E rotates them, except a nearby ready door takes E first. Ctrl + Left/Right selects currents; Ctrl + E moves them. R toggles random encounters outside Marc's forced strong room. Ctrl no longer sinks a diver.

What changed

  • Actual world/maze transitions carry the party's HP, oxygen, items and learned abilities. Maze keys and campaign spell relics are separate.
  • The solved shallow puzzle now leads directly to the maze. Saved completion reopens its doors; maze return places you safely beyond the same entrance. The earlier Deep rock-arch entrance remains an additional compatibility path.
  • The secret encounter is two continuous waves: Angler, Swordfish and Frilled Shark, then Bomb Bot and Sword Slayer. No intermediate healing, reward or victory. Defeating both awards one maze key without clearing the laboratory guards.
  • The main maze sigil starts campaign Cordys with normal combat resolution, separate from his overwhelming opening fight. Tethys remains in the laboratory.
  • Existing volume controls remain. Maze exploration, ordinary/puppet battle music and Cordys music use the existing audio owner; no music restart between puppet waves.
  • Recent Marc integration includes responsive pause/map controls, combat-safe queued help, persistent Bleed and readable status durations, and immediate Evasion Down reduction of the remaining dodge pool. Ready doors take map E before wall rotation and are reachable near their visible top.
