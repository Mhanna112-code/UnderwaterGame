QT11: completing or skipping the starter must persist tutorial completion separately from the already completed opening. A new process must load into a controllable world, not repeat videos/Cordys or force another lesson.

The prepare process writes a declared recovered-player fixture into an unused test slot, loads it through the real title path, swims into the actual training beam, and drives the real quick lesson or visible Skip. It checks the resulting saved milestones and leaves a witness. A second Godot process uses Load Game for that same file, checks the actual UI/route and subsequent W movement, then removes only the fixture files it owns. No injected won result, stat inflation or substituted QTE result. The recovered opening fixture is not proof of the whole initial cinematic flow; existing opening journey checks cover that separately.

A helper refactor that preserves these results should pass. Two decision paths, complete and skip, share the same round-trip oracle. Legacy flag normalization is covered by opening_prologue_state.gd rather than duplicating its table here.
