# Slice 5 — combat/content reconciliation bug catalog

Baseline: `e62e0686615dae77089efec7aada58c1aeb556e6` on
`release/today-build`, after an isolated Godot class-cache scan.  The full
gate exposed one source error and one browser-harness error; neither is being
treated as a reason to omit raw #72 behavior.

| ID | Plausible failure / current observation | Player impact | Cheapest regression contract | Initial state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CR-1 | `CombatRules.resolve()` applies `self_temporary` before accuracy and formulas. A 3-accuracy caster's accuracy-scaled effect is resolved at 2 when the move itself charges -1 ACC. | A move can miss or have a shorter/weaker effect than its displayed/current-stat rule promises. | A self-cost is absent during this move's hit and formula resolution, then present for the attacker's following turn. | **Red:** current resolver applies it first. |
| CR-2 | `Diver.play_death_fade()` queues the party actor for deletion, even though `Tidal Revival` targets downed party members. | Revive can restore HP without restoring a visible, controllable stage actor; later battle UI can retain invalid references. | A downed diver fades and stays valid; a revive returns opacity, scale, vertical position, idle motion, and card visibility. | **Red:** current actor is freed and has no revive method. |
| CR-3 | `Battle._send_home()` assigns `entry.actor` to a typed `Node3D` before validity checking. The full baseline gate emitted `Trying to assign invalid previously freed instance` at `battle.gd:3597`. | An attack finishing while battle cleanup removes its actor can emit Godot errors and leave movement cleanup nondeterministic. | Calling return-home with a freed actor is a quiet no-op; normal valid actors still tween home. | **Red:** source error reproduced by full gate. |
| CR-4 | Raw #72 adds a 3-turn cap to Scuba Bleed and a Flash Blast -1 ACC/EVA cost. Glassgoat's supplied move table states Bleed amount and effect duration where specified, but does not approve either addition. | Blindly porting either changes the authored kit and route balance. | Versioned move-rule table names Glassgoat source, current rule, raw #72 rule, and disposition; balance remains green after every accepted change. | Decision required; do not silently port. |
| CR-5 | Raw #72 removes Ramming Bite and leaves only Bite; #73/current gives Angler Bite, Ramming Bite, Headbutt, and Flash Blast with supplied animations/formulas. | Porting raw data would delete already-playable authored enemy variety and change route difficulty. | Every enabled Angler/Swordfish/Frilled Shark move maps to the supplied source table or a written, test-backed balancing decision. | Current authored roster is preserved pending audit. |
| CR-6 | Raw #72 changes Poison Breath from flat Poison to 15% max HP. The currently approved boss table does not establish that scaling. | A late-game boss may become either trivial or unexpectedly lethal. | Boss poison source and target/max-HP formula are written and exercised by `verify/tethys_boss.gd` before any change. | Decision required; do not silently port. |
| CR-7 | Raw #72 reframes special encounters as random/revealed-item prompts rather than physical guardian sites. | This conflicts with the current deliberate Angler/Swordfish artifact encounters. | Guardian sites remain physically reachable and cannot be preempted by random rolls; special minigames stay launchable as their own route. | Deferred to Slice 6, not dropped. |
| CR-8 | `spell_review_webcheck.mjs` clicks the canvas, provoking a headless `WrongDocumentError` from pointer lock after the visual transitions already succeed. | A harness false-positive can mask actual browser regressions and prevents an all-green suite. | The review route is traversed without requesting pointer lock; any other console error still fails the gate. | **Red:** baseline full gate reports that browser error. |
| CR-9 | A freshly created worktree has no ignored Godot global-class cache, so direct `--script` gates parse as if `Battle`, `Diver`, and other project classes do not exist. | A clean checkout reports dozens of false script errors, making the suite non-reproducible. | `verify/gates.sh` performs the normal headless editor scan before direct scripts; a scan error remains a gate failure. | **Red:** initial isolated baseline had 46 false failures until the scan was run manually. |

The catalog separates approved mechanical repairs (CR-1–CR-3) from genuine
product choices (CR-4–CR-7).  The latter require a source mapping or an
explicit design decision; they are not candidates for a stealth port.
